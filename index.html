RPM history (60 s)
  • 3,000–10,000 rpm is a rotation frequency of 50–167 Hz. The ball makes tones at that frequency and at multiples of it (harmonics).
  • Phone microphones are weak below ~100 Hz, so the fundamental may be faint. Auto mode scores each candidate speed by how well it explains the peaks at 1×, 2×, 3×… and is usually right even if the fundamental is missing.
  • If the reading jumps by a whole factor (half or double), choose a fixed mode instead.
  • Hold the phone close to the ball in a quiet room. Echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain are switched off. The mic needs HTTPS (or localhost) on a phone.
